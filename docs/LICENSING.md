files under the licenses above; do not present them as real recordings.

## Responsible use

Talking-head generation can put words in the mouth of a real person. Use portraits and voices only
with the rights and consent of the people shown and heard, follow the license terms of each model,
and label generated media as AI-generated. Nothing here is legal advice.
